# 📝 Writing Templates

> Use these structures when writing. They help organize your thoughts.

---

## 1. "About Myself" Paragraph (A1, 80–100 words)

```
My name is [name]. I am [age] years old. I live in [city], Ukraine.
I work as a [job] / I am a student.
I like [hobby 1] and [hobby 2].
Every day, I [routine 1] and [routine 2].
I am learning English because [reason].
In the future, I want to [goal].
```

## 2. "My Day" Paragraph (A1–A2, 100–120 words)

```
I usually wake up at [time]. First, I [morning routine].
Then I go to [work/school]. I work/study from [time] to [time].
After work, I usually [evening activity].
In the evening, I [activity]. I also like to [hobby].
I go to bed at [time].
Today was [adjective] because [reason].
```

## 3. "Opinion" Paragraph (A2–B1, 120–150 words)

```
I think that [topic/opinion].
First of all, [reason 1 + example].
Also, [reason 2 + example].
On the other hand, some people think [opposite view].
However, I believe [restate opinion].
In conclusion, [summary].
```

## 4. "Informal Email" (A2–B1, 120–150 words)

```
Hi [name],

How are you? I am writing to tell you about [topic].

[Paragraph 1: main information]

[Paragraph 2: details or plans]

I hope to hear from you soon!

Best wishes,
[Your name]
```
