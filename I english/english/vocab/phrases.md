## Day 1 (2026-02-21) — Basic Communication Phrases

1. **How are you?** — Як справи?; A1; — **How are you?** — I'm fine, thanks.
2. **Nice to meet you.** — Приємно познайомитись.; A1; **Nice to meet you**, Anna!
3. **I don't understand.** — Я не розумію.; A1; Sorry, **I don't understand**. Can you repeat?
4. **Can you help me?** — Чи можете ви мені допомогти?; A1; Excuse me, **can you help me**?
5. **I would like...** — Я б хотів...; A2; **I would like** a cup of coffee, please.
