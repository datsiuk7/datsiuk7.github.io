## Month 1 (Feb 21 – Mar 21): Foundations & A2 Solidification

**Grammar targets:**
- Present Simple vs Present Continuous (rules, signals, drills)
- Past Simple (regular/irregular verbs, questions, negatives)
- Articles basics (a/an/the vs zero article)
- Prepositions of time & place (in/on/at)
- Word order in questions and negatives

**Vocab targets:**
- A1 core (200 words): daily life, family, food, time, numbers, basic adjectives
- A2 start (100 words): routines, work, travel, shopping
- 30+ useful phrases

**Writing:**
- Short paragraphs (80–120 words): about myself, my day, my family, my job

**Reading:**
- Simple A1/A2 texts with comprehension questions

| Week | Dates | Grammar Focus | Vocab Theme | Writing Task | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Feb 28–Mar 6 | Present Continuous + contrast with Present Simple | Home, rooms, actions | My typical day (100 words) | Quiz: Simple vs Continuous |
| 3 | Mar 7–13 | Past Simple (regular verbs, was/were) | Food, shopping, prices | Last weekend (100 words) | Quiz: Past Simple sentences |
| 4 | Mar 14–20 | Past Simple (irregular verbs) + questions/negatives | Travel, transport, directions | A trip I took (120 words) | Monthly test: Mixed tenses |

## Month 6 (Jul 22 – Aug 21): B2 Consolidation & Exam Prep

**Grammar targets:**
- Conditional 3 / Mixed conditionals

**Writing:**
- B2-level essays, reports, formal letters (220+ words)

**Final assessment:**
- Grammar test (all tenses + structures)
- Vocabulary test (500+ words recall)
- Writing sample (250 words, timed)
- Translation test (10 complex sentences each way)
