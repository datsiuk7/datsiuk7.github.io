## Вправа 1: Артиклі a/an (Дата: 2026-02-21)

**Завдання:** Встав **a** або **an** де потрібно.

1. I have ___ cat.
2. She is ___ teacher.
3. I want ___ apple.
4. He reads ___ book every day.
5. I need ___ umbrella.

**Відповіді:**
1. a cat
2. a teacher
3. an apple (голосний звук!)
4. a book
5. an umbrella (голосний звук!)

---

## Вправа 2: want to / like + V-ing (Дата: 2026-02-21)

**Завдання:** Виправи речення.

1. I want watch a movie. → ___
2. She like swim. → ___
3. We want go home. → ___
4. He like play football. → ___
5. I want be a programmer. → ___

**Відповіді:**
1. I want **to** watch a movie.
2. She like**s** swimming. / She likes to swim.
3. We want **to** go home.
4. He like**s** playing football. / He likes to play football.
5. I want **to** be a programmer.

---

## Вправа 3: he/she/it + s (Дата: 2026-02-21)

**Завдання:** Постав дієслово в правильну форму.

1. He (eat) ___ breakfast at 8.
2. She (work) ___ every day.
3. It (rain) ___ a lot in autumn.
4. My cat (like) ___ fish.
5. My brother (go) ___ to school.

**Відповіді:**
1. He **eats** breakfast at 8.
2. She **works** every day.
3. It **rains** a lot in autumn.
4. My cat **likes** fish.
5. My brother **goes** to school.

---

## Вправа 4: Прийменники часу at/in/on (Дата: 2026-02-21)

**Завдання:** Встав at, in або on.

1. I wake up ___ 7 o'clock.
2. I study ___ the morning.
3. We have a meeting ___ Monday.
4. She was born ___ 1998.
5. I eat lunch ___ noon.

**Відповіді:**
1. **at** 7 o'clock
2. **in** the morning
3. **on** Monday
4. **in** 1998
5. **at** noon
