## Overview Table

| Tense | Formula | Example | Signal Words | UA Equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | S + V(s) | I work. / He works. | always, every day, usually | Я працюю. |
| **Present Continuous** | S + am/is/are + V-ing | I am working. | now, at the moment | Я (зараз) працюю. |
| **Past Simple** | S + V-ed / V2 | I worked. / I went. | yesterday, last week, ago | Я працював. |
| **Past Continuous** | S + was/were + V-ing | I was working. | while, at that time | Я працював (у той момент). |
| **Future Simple** | S + will + V | I will work. | tomorrow, next week | Я буду працювати. |
| **Going to** | S + am/is/are + going to + V | I am going to work. | plan, intention | Я збираюся працювати. |
| **Present Perfect** | S + have/has + V3 | I have worked. | already, yet, just, ever, never, for, since | Я (вже) працював / попрацював. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | S + have/has + been + V-ing | I have been working. | for, since, how long | Я працюю (вже якийсь час). |
| **Past Perfect** | S + had + V3 | I had worked. | before, after, by the time | Я (вже) був попрацював. |
| **Future Perfect** | S + will have + V3 | I will have worked. | by tomorrow, by then | Я вже буду мати зробленим. |
